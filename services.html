Questions

What clients ask us first.

Can we engage a single service line?

Yes. Most clients start with business strategy planning or management consultancy, then expand. The mobile app stays with you either way, and each business line can be switched on as it becomes relevant.

Is the mobile app required?

No, but we recommend it. The app is how delivery, orders, inventory and supply chain tracking stay visible after the engagement ends — it is the handover, not a dependency.

Which markets do you cover?

Our base is Hong Kong, and we work regularly across Mainland China, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand, Japan, South Korea, the United Kingdom and the European Union.

How do you handle compliance and trade control?

Sanctions screening, customs documentation and export control sit inside our general trading and international trade service lines. See our Terms of Service for the legal framework that governs it.

Do you work with existing internal teams?

Always. We are not a replacement for your people — we build the model with them so the capability stays in the business when we step back.

What does an engagement cost?

Advisory work is scoped by phase and priced against deliverables; trading and supply chain work is priced per programme. We quote after the diagnostic conversation, in writing.
